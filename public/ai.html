How I work with AI.

AI is part of every stage of my process, from the first benchmark to the handoff. It makes the work faster and broader. The questions, the decisions and the time with users stay mine.

The process

Five stages, one workflow

Each stage has a clear split: what I do, where AI helps, and what I never hand over.

01 · BEFORE DESIGNING

Benchmark

What I do

I map how other banks and SaaS products solve the same problem: patterns, entry points, wording, edge cases.

Where AI helps

AI scans and structures the references into a side-by-side comparison and flags the patterns that repeat.

What stays mine

Which references matter for our users and constraints, and what we deliberately do differently.

02 · BEFORE DESIGNING

UX audits

What I do

I audit the current flow against usability heuristics, accessibility and the design system, screen by screen.

Where AI helps

A Claude project holds the full product context, so it can review a flow, list issues by severity and point to the rule each one breaks.

What stays mine

Prioritising what is worth fixing now, and checking every finding against what real users actually do.

03 · DESIGNING

Design with the system

What I do

With Claude connected to Figma through MCP, I generate screens and variants built from the real design system components and tokens.

Where AI helps

It assembles layouts, explores variants of the same screen and fixes inconsistencies in the system itself, inside the Figma file.

What stays mine

The direction, the hierarchy and the final pick. Variants are a way to compare options faster, not to decide for me.

04 · TESTING

Prototypes in code

What I do

I build working prototypes in Astro and Three.js with Cursor and Claude Code.

Where AI helps

It writes and iterates the code, so the team gets something to open, click and test much earlier.

What stays mine

What the prototype needs to prove, and what we learn from people using it.

05 · SHIPPING

Handoff

What I do

I prepare the handoff with development: specs, states, edge cases and acceptance notes for each screen.

Where AI helps

It drafts the documentation from the Figma file and the decisions log, and checks that no state or edge case is missing.

What stays mine

Reviewing every spec with the developers, and the visual QA of what gets built.

In practice

The design system in the loop

One source of truth

Designs and variants are generated with the same components and tokens the team ships, so what I explore is already buildable.

A system that stays clean

Through the Figma connector I find and fix inconsistencies in the library: detached instances, wrong tokens, missing states.

Context that travels

A project with the product context, rules and decisions means audits and documents start from what we already know.

The line I keep

AI speeds up the work. The problem framing, the trade-offs, the taste and the conversations with users and developers are still the job, and still mine.
